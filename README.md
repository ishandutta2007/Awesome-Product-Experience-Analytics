# Awesome-Product-Experience-Analytics

## Top Product Experience Analytics Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Product Analytics, Session Replay, Feature Adoption, Funnels, Cohorts & Digital Experience Insights*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Product Experience Analytics**. These tools help product and growth teams understand user behavior, measure feature adoption, analyze funnels and retention, capture session replays, and improve the overall product experience.



**Examples** include Pendo, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Heap, FullStory, Contentsquare, Quantum Metric, PostHog, UXCam, and Glassbox (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Product analytics has a strong open-source leader in **PostHog** (self-hostable analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and more). Additional open options include Matomo, Countly, and related privacy-focused tools. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Pendo](https://www.pendo.io/)**  

  Product experience platform combining analytics, in-app guidance, feedback, and roadmapping to improve adoption and user outcomes.



- **[Amplitude](https://amplitude.com/)**  

  Leading behavioral product analytics platform focused on deep cohort analysis, retention, experimentation, and path insights.



- **[Mixpanel](https://mixpanel.com/)**  

  Event-based product analytics platform known for clear funnels, retention reports, and accessible self-serve analysis.



- **[Heap](https://www.heap.io/)**  

  Autocapture-oriented product analytics platform that automatically tracks user interactions for retrospective analysis.



- **[FullStory](https://www.fullstory.com/)**  

  Digital experience and session replay platform providing detailed playback, frustration signals, and behavioral insights.



- **[Contentsquare](https://contentsquare.com/)**  

  Digital experience analytics platform focused on journey analysis, heatmaps, and optimization insights across web and apps.



- **[Quantum Metric](https://www.quantummetric.com/)**  

  Continuous product design and experience analytics platform with session replay and real-time insights.



- **[PostHog](https://posthog.com/)**  

  Open-source product analytics platform (also offered as cloud) combining analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and more.



- **[UXCam](https://uxcam.com/)**  

  Mobile app analytics and session replay platform focused on understanding user behavior in native applications.



- **[Glassbox](https://www.glassbox.com/)**  

  Digital experience analytics and session replay platform used for web and mobile journey analysis.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[PostHog](https://github.com/posthog/posthog)**  

  Leading open-source product analytics platform. Provides event analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, surveys, error tracking, and more. Fully self-hostable with a generous free cloud tier.



- **[Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**  

  Open-source web and product analytics platform focused on privacy and data ownership. Can be self-hosted as an alternative to commercial analytics tools.



- **[Countly](https://github.com/Countly/countly-server)**  

  Open-source analytics platform for mobile, web, and desktop applications with product and performance insights.



- **[Plausible and privacy-focused open analytics](https://github.com/plausible/analytics)**  

  Lightweight, privacy-oriented open-source web analytics that can complement product analytics stacks.



- **[OpenPanel and emerging open product analytics](https://github.com/)**  

  Newer open-source product analytics projects aiming for modern event tracking and self-hosting.



- **[Session replay open components](https://github.com/)**  

  Open libraries and tools for capturing and replaying user sessions (often integrated into broader platforms like PostHog).



- **[Feature flag and experimentation open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open feature-flag and A/B testing frameworks that pair well with product analytics.



- **[Event tracking and CDP open pipelines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open tools for collecting, transforming, and routing product events into analytics warehouses.



- **[Heatmap and visual analytics open projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools for generating heatmaps and visual interaction insights.



- **[SQL and warehouse-native analytics open helpers](https://github.com/)**  

  Approaches that use open query engines and dashboards on top of product event data stored in warehouses.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with **PostHog** for a complete, self-hostable product analytics + session replay + feature-flag stack.

- Using **Matomo** or **Countly** when privacy and full data ownership are primary requirements.

- Combining open analytics with a data warehouse for advanced custom analysis.

- Accepting that enterprise-scale behavioral modeling, advanced autocapture, specialized mobile SDKs, and polished in-app guidance still favor commercial platforms (Amplitude, Mixpanel, Pendo, FullStory, Contentsquare, etc.).

- Deploying PostHog (self-hosted or cloud) as the core and selectively adding commercial tools for specific capabilities.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Instrument events (manual or autocapture) → send to PostHog or another open analytics backend → analyze funnels, retention, and paths → add session replay and feature flags → run experiments. This provides a fully open product experience analytics foundation. Commercial platforms remain the practical choice for teams that want managed infrastructure, deeper enterprise features, or specialized digital-experience capabilities.
